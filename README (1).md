## About

I'm a Threat Hunter at **TCS**, focused on investigating suspicious activity, developing hunting hypotheses, and translating threat intelligence into practical investigations.

My work spans endpoint and identity-focused hunting, campaign analysis, and security telemetry across Microsoft and CrowdStrike environments. I enjoy understanding how adversaries operate, validating their techniques against available telemetry, and documenting findings in a way that helps security teams act on them.

- 🔎 **Hunting:** Hypothesis-driven and campaign-based threat hunting
- 🧭 **Intelligence:** Threat actor activity, intrusion campaigns, and ATT&CK mapping
- 🪟 **Identity & endpoint:** Active Directory, Windows telemetry, and endpoint investigations
- 🛠️ **Detection:** KQL, hunting queries, and detection-use-case development
- 📚 **Research:** Vulnerability analysis, technical write-ups, and hands-on labs
